# superstore-sales-analysis (SQL)  

## Overview
This repository contains an exploratory data analysis of the Superstore dataset using SQLite. The project focuses on assessing sales performance, order volumes, customer purchasing behavior, and shipping timeline patterns to answer core business questions.

## Dataset & Tools
* **Database:** SQLite (`superstore_sales.db`)
* **Language:** SQL
* **Tools Used:** DB Browser for SQLite

## Key Business Questions
    1. Which product categories generate the highest total sales and order counts?
    2. Who are the top 10 customers by total purchasing volume?
    3. How are total sales distributed across different geographic regions?
    4. How are customers segmented based on their spending behavior (High, Medium, Low spenders)
    5. What is the monthly sales trend, and are there visible seasonal patterns over time?

## Main Insights
* **Top Category:** Technology generated the highest overall revenue.
* **Geographic Spread:** The West region accounts for the highest total sales volume, with Chairs exceeding $100K.
* **Customer Value:** A small percentage of High-Spender customers accounts for a significant portion of total sales.
* **Monthly Trends:** Sales volume shows clear seasonal spikes toward the end of the year (Q4).
